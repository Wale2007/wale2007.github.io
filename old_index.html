05 // Project

Whale Watch

A full-stack DeFi trading and on-chain intelligence platform — built under the DPRINCE brand with the tagline Trade. Track. Protect. It combines a live token trading interface with a dedicated whale intelligence dashboard that monitors large wallet movements across ETH, BNB and more in real time.

The Trade tab works like a DEX aggregator: paste any token contract address, select your chain, and instantly see live price, market cap, liquidity, 24h volume, transaction count, and an interactive candlestick chart with 5M, 15M, 1H, 4H and 1D timeframes. A built-in Security Scan analyses each token for honeypot risk, blacklist status, mint functions, and buy/sell tax — giving it a safety score out of 100. Top holders are also displayed with their percentage ownership.

The Whale Intelligence tab tracks large wallet movements across Ethereum and BNB Chain in real time — showing total transaction count, total ETH volume moved, DEX swaps vs direct transfers, and the number of wallets being watched. Transactions are filterable by type: All Moves, DEX Swaps, or Transfers. Each entry shows the wallet name, chain, transaction type, amount, destination address, timestamp and a direct link to the block explorer.
